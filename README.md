# Fine Organic Industries Limited — Financial Modeling & Valuation

## Overview

A comprehensive financial modeling and fundamental analysis project on **Fine Organic Industries Limited (FOIL)**, a specialty chemicals company focused on oleochemical-based additives.

The project combines company and industry research with historical financial analysis, financial projections, and valuation analysis to develop an analyst-style view of the company's financial performance and valuation.

## Project Scope

### 1. Company & Industry Analysis

* Company and business overview
* Product and application analysis
* Manufacturing footprint and capacity
* Oleochemicals industry overview
* Market outlook and growth drivers
* Industry risks and challenges
* Raw material trends

### 2. Historical Financial Analysis

* Historical Income Statement analysis
* Balance Sheet analysis
* Working Capital analysis
* Financial ratio analysis
* Revenue, profitability and margin trends
* Analysis of key business and financial drivers

### 3. Financial Modeling & Forecasting

* Operating assumptions and key business drivers
* Capacity and utilization analysis
* Revenue projections
* Cost and profitability projections
* Capital expenditure assumptions
* Projected financial statements
* Working capital projections
* Key financial metrics and ratios

### 4. Valuation Analysis

* Comparable Company Method (CCM)
* Peer company analysis and EV/EBITDA multiples
* Maintainable EBITDA calculation
* Market Price Method
* Enterprise value and implied valuation analysis

For the CCM, maintainable EBITDA is derived using the average of TTM Dec-25 adjusted EBITDA and FY25 adjusted EBITDA, with the selected EV/EBITDA multiple derived from the comparable-company analysis.
